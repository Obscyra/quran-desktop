# Features

Everything Quran for Desktop can do. For a short overview see the [README](../README.md).

## Reading
- **Two ways to read**, switched from the top bar: *Verse by Verse* (each verse with its translation and tools) and *Reading* (the printed Mushaf, or the translation alone), the same split as [quran.com](https://quran.com).
- **Mushaf pages** with a **two-page spread** that reads right to left like an Arabic book (page 2 on the right, page 3 on its left), single pages with a page turn, or one long scroll. Page turns glide, and long surahs open without freezing the window.
- **Seven script styles with a live preview:** Madani Mushaf, Tajweed (colour-coded rules), Uthmani, IndoPak, Naskh, Amiri Quran and Scheherazade.
- **Zoom, Fit and Fill for the Mushaf pages** (in both Pages and Scroll): Fit shows the whole page, Fill uses the full width of the window, and the zoom buttons go from 50% to 300% on top of either. A page bigger than the window scrolls, and the mouse wheel turns the page once you reach its edge.
- **Page types:** Madani (15 lines, 604 pages) and IndoPak (15 lines, 610 pages), each with a Paper, Frame or Plain look.
- **Translations:** 100+ in dozens of languages, plus transliteration and word-by-word meanings, which follow the translation's language.
- **Bookmarks** with notes, a marked ornament on bookmarked verses, jump to any verse by typing `55:13`, and export or import.
- **Back and forward** history through the surahs you opened, with the arrows in the title bar or the side buttons of your mouse.

## Listening
- **17 reciters that follow along**, and 90+ more that play whole surahs without highlighting (grouped as "Listen only" in the list), with portraits (bundled, 27 of the listen-only ones share them), speed control, repeat and automatic next surah.
- **Word-by-word tracking** that follows the recitation, with a smooth sweep inside each word.
- **Play one verse or one word.** Click a word to hear it, or a verse number to hear the whole verse. Right-click either one for its meaning, play from here, repeat, bookmark or copy.
- **Change reciter mid-surah** and the new voice carries on from the same verse.
- **One-click Play** in the title bar. The player bar opens when you start listening and closes with its cross.
- **Right-click any surah** in the list to read it, listen to it, download or remove its audio, or copy its name.

## Offline
- **The whole Quran is in the installer:** the Arabic text of all 114 surahs (with Tajweed markup and IndoPak spelling) and all 604 Mushaf page fonts. Reading needs no connection. Only audio and other translations come from the internet. Once a week, while online, the app compares the provider's text and page fonts with its own and keeps any corrections.
- **Audio manager:** pick a reciter, then download or delete any surah, or all 114 at once, with a live percentage for each.
- **Download status at a glance:** an icon in the player shows whether the surah is saved, downloading (with its percentage) or still online.
- Recently used surahs are cached, and the next surah is prepared while one plays.
- **Favourites stay offline by themselves:** a surah you listen to on three different days in a month is saved automatically, and removed again after a month without listening. Recitations you download yourself are never removed.
- A loading indicator shows when the connection is slow, instead of leaving the app looking frozen.

## Comfort
- **Themes:** thirteen. Dark: Night, Tahajjud, Tide, Fjord, Graphite, Moss, Sidr, Rosewood, Madinah and Slate. Light: Paper, White & Gold and Stone. Or follow the system.
- **First-run setup** (every step can be skipped) for theme, size, reading mode, script, language and word meanings, and a **search bar in Settings** that jumps to any option.
- **Interface size** from 90% to 150%, or any percentage you type (50–200%), independent of the Arabic text size. The interface font (Inter) and the Arabic fonts (Amiri Quran, Noto Naskh Arabic, Scheherazade New) are bundled with the app, so it looks the same on every machine.
- **Full screen**, with the screen kept awake while you listen. The window opens at the size and place you left it.
- **Player bar on hover (optional):** hide the bar until the mouse reaches the bottom edge, so the page keeps all its room.
- **Lock on the playing verse:** in the scrolling views, scrolling away only lasts a moment and the view returns to the verse being recited, kept whole on screen (or the lines being recited, when the verse is taller than the window). With the lock off, an arrow in the corner takes you back to the verse.
- **One window, remembered:** Quran runs as a single instance (opening it again brings the running window back from the tray), and it reopens at the size and place you left it.
- **Runs in the system tray:** closing the window keeps Quran in the tray (turn it off in Settings), and it can start with your computer, so **reminders** and the **Verse of the Day** from quran.com arrive as system notifications even when the window is closed.
- **Tips that help, not nag:** a small card offers a matching setting when you keep doing what it is for (two pages side by side, a translation, scroll mode, zoom and Fit/Fill, a floating player). At most one a day, and **Show tips** in Settings turns them off.
- **Playback from outside the window:** on Windows, Previous, Play/Pause and Next sit under the preview that opens when you hover the taskbar icon; on macOS and Linux the system's media controls and media keys work too.
- **In-app updates:** new versions download in the background and install when you restart (Settings → Updates).

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action | Key | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Space` | Play or pause | `T` | Two-page spread |
| `←` `→` | Turn the page, or step verses while listening | `L` | Listening bar |
| `Page Up` `Page Down` | Previous or next page | `B` | Surah list |
| `P` | Verse by Verse or Reading | `F` | Full screen |
| `R` | Arabic or Translation | `,` | Settings |
| `Esc` | Close a menu or leave Settings | | |
